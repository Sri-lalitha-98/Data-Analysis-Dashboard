# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Objective

Blinkit (formerly Grofers), India's Last Minute App, 
wanted to analyze its outlet sales performance to 
understand which product categories, outlet types, 
locations, and customer preferences drive the most 
revenue — enabling data-driven decisions to optimize 
inventory, expand outlet presence, and boost sales 
growth.

## 📂 Dataset Used

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| 📁 **File** | [BlinkIT Grocery Data.xlsx](https://github.com/Sri-lalitha-98/Data-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/BlinkIT%20Grocery%20Data.xlsx) |
| 🌐 **Source** | Blinkit Grocery Sales Dataset |
| 📊 **Records** | 8,523+ items |
| 🗂️ **Fields** | Item Type, Fat Content, Outlet Size, Outlet Type, Outlet Location (Tier), Establishment Year, Sales, Rating, Item Visibility |

## ❓ Questions (KPIs)

- 📊 What is the total revenue, average sales, 
  and average customer rating?
- 🛍️ Which item types generate the highest sales?
- 🧈 Do customers prefer Low Fat or Regular products?
- 🏪 Which outlet type contributes the most 
  to total sales?
- 📐 How does outlet size impact total revenue?
- 📍 Which city tier (Tier 1/2/3) generates 
  maximum sales?
- 📈 How has outlet establishment year affected 
  sales over time?
- 👁️ What is the item visibility vs sales 
  relationship?
- ✅ What percentage of orders were successfully 
  delivered?

  dashboard with slicers for **Outlet Location Type**,

## 💡 Project Insights

**1. 🧈 Fat Content Preference**
> Regular fat products ($778K) sell more than 
> Low Fat products ($425K). Customers choose 
> taste over healthy options.

**2. 🥦 Top Selling Item Categories**
> Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods are the 
> best sellers at $0.18M each. Daily essentials 
> dominate Blinkit sales.

**3. 🏪 Outlet Type Performance**
> Supermarket Type1 leads with $787K in sales. 
> It is 5x higher than any other outlet type.

**4. 📐 Outlet Size Impact**
> Larger outlets earn more —
> - High size → $508K
> - Medium size → $445K  
> - Small size → $249K

**5. 📍 Location Tier Analysis**
> Tier 3 cities sell the most. Small cities have 
> less competition from offline stores, so 
> Blinkit performs better there.

**6. 📈 Outlet Establishment Trend**
> Sales peaked between 2018–2020 at $205K. 
> After 2020, growth became stable showing 
> Blinkit is now a matured brand.

**7. 👁️ Item Visibility**
> Products placed more visibly on shelves sell 
> better. Good placement drives more purchases.

## ✅ Final Conclusion

> To grow Blinkit's sales, 
> here are the key focus areas:

**1. 🏪 Expand Supermarket Type1 in Tier 3 Cities**
> Tier 3 cities + Supermarket Type1 is the best 
> combination for highest sales returns.

**2. 🛍️ Promote Top Selling Products**
> Run flash deals and combo offers on Regular fat 
> products, Snacks and Fresh produce through 
> the Blinkit app.

**3. 🏗️ Invest in Larger Outlets**
> Build bigger stores in semi-urban areas where 
> customer demand is growing but stores 
> are limited.

**4. 📱 Run Targeted Digital Campaigns**
> Focus ads on daily essentials like Fruits, 
> Snacks and Household items during peak 
> ordering hours in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

**5. 👁️ Improve Product Visibility**
> Give better shelf placement to Dairy, Canned 
> and Baking Goods — these categories have 
> hidden sales potential.
